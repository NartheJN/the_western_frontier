## Overview
The **Firbolg Tribes of Greywood** are the local Firbolg communities that inhabit the forests and surrounding lands beyond Eswick. They have lived in the region since long before the arrival of the Empire and maintain a close relationship with the land they call home.

## Culture
The tribes share many customs and traditions, though each maintains its own practices and identity. Their communities place importance on cooperation, respect for the land, and maintaining the balance of their surroundings.

## Tribal Structure
The Tribes of Greywood are not a single unified nation. Each tribe maintains its own community and leadership, while matters that affect the tribes as a whole may be discussed between them. Their relationships with one another are shaped by shared ancestry, traditions, and their connection to Greywood.

## Relationship with the Land
The tribes regard the forests and surrounding wilderness as more than undeveloped territory. Hunting grounds, travel routes, settlements, and other places may carry significance that is not immediately apparent to outsiders. Their knowledge of the region allows them to move through areas that remain unfamiliar to the people of Eswick.

## Relationship with Eswick
The relationship between the Tribes of Greywood and Eswick is peaceful but complicated. The two groups maintain contact and coexist in the same region, but the history of conflict has left a degree of distrust on both sides. The tribes remain wary of further expansion by the town, while Eswick continues to rely on its scouts to monitor the surrounding lands.

## History
The Tribes of Greywood lived in the region for generations before the arrival of the Imperial expedition. Initial contact with the newcomers was cautious but peaceful, but tensions developed as the settlement expanded into lands the tribes already used and considered their own.

Disputes over land, hunting, the burning of Greywood and the growth of the settlement gradually led to violence. The conflict reached its worst point with the destruction of what is now Scar-Town, after which the displaced expedition took refuge in the Arcanists' tower.

Following the destruction of the settlement, both sides reconsidered the cost of continuing the conflict. Negotiations eventually led to the **Greywater Accords**, which established a formal peace and set limits on the expansion of the settlement into Firbolg territory.

The Accords ended the open fighting but did not erase the distrust created by the conflict. The history of Scar-Town and the events that followed continue to shape the relationship between Eswick and the Tribes of Greywood.

## Notable Tribes
The Tribes of Greywood consist of several distinct Firbolg tribes. Each has its own identity, customs, and territory within the wider region, while remaining connected through their shared heritage and the lands of Greywood.
