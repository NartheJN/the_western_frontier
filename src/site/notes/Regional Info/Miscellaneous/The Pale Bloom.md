The Pale Bloom is an unfamiliar disease first encountered in the wilderness beyond Eswick and later carried back to the town by returning explorers. Its early symptoms can resemble an ordinary illness, but distinctive pale markings soon begin to appear beneath the skin, spreading in branching patterns across the chest, neck, and limbs.

Those afflicted often experience severe sensory disturbances as the disease progresses. Food and drink may take on strange or unpleasant flavours, ordinary sounds can seem distant or distorted, and bright light may become difficult to tolerate. A persistent dry cough usually follows, with more advanced cases producing a fine, pale dust from the lungs that can infect others through close exposure.

As the illness worsens, the victim becomes increasingly weak and disoriented. Some lose their sense of direction or wander aimlessly, apparently unaware of their surroundings. The disease eventually runs its course, but serious cases can be fatal, and its unfamiliar symptoms made the outbreak particularly frightening to those who witnessed it.

The origin of the Pale Bloom remains uncertain. The explorers who first brought it to Eswick encountered no obvious source for the disease, and no definitive explanation for its appearance has ever been established.
